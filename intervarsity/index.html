I believe leadership is about coming alongside people, seeing their potential, and helping them lead with courage.

With a degree in Healthcare Administration, I began my career in healthcare. In 2011, I left that work to serve students and faculty with InterVarsity at the University of North Texas and Texas Woman’s University. That step grew into opportunities to serve teams at the area, regional, and national levels.

Today, I also partner with executives and leadership teams through fractional leadership, coaching, and strategic advising. My Christian faith shapes how I serve: with encouragement, thoughtful stewardship, and a commitment to people.

01 Fractional executive leadership +

Senior leadership support without a full-time executive engagement.

  • Chief of Staff: align priorities, coordinate leadership, and move key initiatives forward.
  • Chief Operating Officer: strengthen systems, workflows, accountability, and resource planning.
  • Hybrid executive partner: connect strategic direction with operational execution.
02 Executive & leadership coaching +

Individual and team coaching focused on leadership growth, decision-making, trust, and navigating change. Available through virtual, in-person, and hybrid engagements.

03 Strategic planning & facilitation +

Make room for clear thinking. Strategy sessions, leadership retreats, and organizational planning help teams clarify direction and agree on practical next steps.

04 Training, speaking & retreats +

Tailored workshops and speaking on leadership, team culture, communication, and ministry. Sessions can serve individuals, teams, cohorts, and conference or retreat gatherings.
