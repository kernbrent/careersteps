Where the story began

A change of career.
A step of faith.

In 2011, a stable healthcare career gave way to a new direction: full-time ministry with university students and faculty.

The invitation to serve at UNT and TWU brought both anxiety and excitement. In his InterVarsity story, Benzi describes a deep sense of calling to equip people to follow Jesus and become a catalyst for future leaders.

His education in Healthcare Administration belongs to the same story. The move into ministry changed his setting, while his concern for people, leadership, and the way organizations work continued to find new expression.

The journey in ministry

Faithful steps.
Widening responsibility.

  1. BEFORE 2011

    A foundation in healthcare

    With a degree in Healthcare Administration and a career in healthcare, Benzi’s early professional path began in a setting where people and organizational systems meet.

  2. 2011

    A step onto campus

    Benzi left his healthcare role to become a campus staff member with InterVarsity at the University of North Texas and Texas Woman’s University. He describes a strong calling to empower students and faculty to champion the cause of Christ.

  3. 2014

    Serving Greater North Texas

    As Area Director for State Universities of Greater North Texas, Benzi led a team of paid staff and volunteers through the demanding and rewarding work of rebuilding ministry in the area.

  4. 2020

    Regional strategy & leadership

    As Associate Regional Director in the Red River Region, he helped lead directors and coordinators across four states. His work connected missional vision, strategic planning, staff leadership, and regional initiatives.

  5. NATIONAL LEADERSHIP

    Supporting the wider movement

    His InterVarsity profile describes his role as National Associate Chief of Staff and Senior Field Director. He works with field-ministry executive leadership and helps oversee national departments and projects.

The work behind the scenes

The spirit behind the service

Encouragement
is leadership.

In his coaching materials, Benzi points to Barnabas as a model of leadership: someone who believes in others, advocates for people, and encourages them to stay faithful to their calling.

That same conviction connects his ministry and consulting. He wants to help people recognize their potential and gain the clarity and courage to move forward.

“My leadership philosophy is to pray, plan, and play.”— Benzi George
