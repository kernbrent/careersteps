Meet Benzi George

People first.
Purpose always.
Progress together.

Executive partner, leadership coach, and ministry leader. A background in Healthcare Administration and a calling to help others lead well.

A connected story

The work changes.
The heart carries through.

Benzi’s work spans healthcare, campus ministry, organizational leadership, and executive partnership.

He holds a degree in Healthcare Administration and worked in healthcare before joining InterVarsity in 2011. His ministry path began at UNT and TWU, grew into area leadership in Greater North Texas, and later included regional strategy and national field ministry responsibilities.

Alongside his ministry, his consulting materials describe fractional Chief of Staff and COO services, executive coaching, strategic planning, team training, speaking, and retreat facilitation.

Across those settings, a consistent theme emerges: encouraging people, clarifying direction, and building the practical structures that help a team move forward.

A philosophy for leadership

Pray. Plan. Play.

Pray

Faith is part of Benzi’s foundation. Prayer creates space for discernment, humility, and attention to a purpose larger than the immediate task.

Plan

Good intentions need a way forward. Thoughtful priorities, clear responsibilities, and practical rhythms help people turn a shared vision into action.

Play

Joy belongs in leadership, too. Relationships, encouragement, and room to enjoy one another can help sustain the people doing demanding work.

A model of encouragement

Coming alongside
the next leader.

Benzi names Barnabas as an important example in his approach to coaching. Barnabas’s encouragement, advocacy, and willingness to invest in others give shape to Benzi’s desire to help leaders grow into their calling.

His consulting and ministry share this relational starting point, while serving different needs. Organizations can explore practical executive support and coaching. Ministry partners can connect around the campus mission through InterVarsity.
