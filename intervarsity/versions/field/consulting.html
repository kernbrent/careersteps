Executive capacity, shared over time

What does
fractional mean?

A fractional executive serves your organization for an agreed portion of their working time, rather than in a full-time role.

The engagement can provide continuing leadership capacity: helping set priorities, coordinating people, and staying involved as plans are put into practice. Time, responsibilities, decision-making authority, and meeting rhythms are agreed together.

For example, a team might explore an executive partner for a set number of hours each week while it strengthens its operating systems or navigates a transition. Benzi’s materials describe ongoing and short-term arrangements, including engagements around ten hours per week. The right fit depends on the work and his availability.

People and systems.
Seen together.

Benzi holds a degree in Healthcare Administration and worked in healthcare before entering full-time campus ministry. That background provides a useful starting point for conversations about how organizations support people and organize work.

For healthcare and other service organizations, these are administrative consulting areas to explore with Benzi. A specific engagement would be shaped around his experience, your team’s expertise, and the scope agreed together.

Operations & process improvement

Explore how work moves between people and departments. Map handoffs, clarify responsibilities, and identify avoidable delays or duplicated effort.

Resource & budget planning

Connect priorities to staffing, time, and available resources. Establish practical budget-review habits with the organization’s finance team.

People & workforce planning

Clarify roles, onboarding, management rhythms, and staff-development priorities so teams understand what is expected and where they can grow.

Performance & reporting

Identify a useful set of administrative measures, agree on who owns them, and build a regular rhythm for reviewing progress and making decisions.

Change & project coordination

Give improvement efforts structure: an owner, a realistic sequence, stakeholder communication, and an agreed method for tracking implementation.

Organizational readiness

Help teams document processes, coordinate policy implementation, and organize follow-up with their internal subject-matter experts.

Questions you may be carrying

Before we begin.

Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Begin with the challenge you are facing. A conversation can help clarify whether coaching, a project, or fractional leadership is a suitable fit.

Is this only for faith-based organizations?

The service conversation can include businesses, nonprofits, churches, and ministries. Benzi’s Christian faith shapes his approach; the goals, language, and format of the engagement should reflect the organization and its context.

Does a fractional executive replace our team?

The role is designed around agreed responsibilities and works alongside existing leaders. Reporting lines and decision rights should be clear from the outset.

What should we bring to an introductory conversation?

A short picture of your organization, the most important challenge, your desired timeframe, and what progress would look like. A finished brief is not required.
