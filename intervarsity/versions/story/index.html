The heart behind the work

Different settings.
The same heart.

For me, leadership begins with a relationship.

Whether I’m working alongside an executive team or serving leaders in campus ministry, I want to help people see what is possible—and find a practical way forward.

My path has taken me from healthcare into InterVarsity ministry, and from local campuses into regional and national leadership. Today, those experiences also shape my work as an executive partner and coach.

“My leadership philosophy is to pray, plan, and play.”— Benzi George
