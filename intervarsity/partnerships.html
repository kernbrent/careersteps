Partnerships

Meaningful work.
Shared purpose.
Stronger together.

Leadership and ministry grow through relationships. These connections bring together campus ministry, practical consulting, and opportunities to serve alongside others.

People and organizations, connected

Different strengths.
A shared heart to serve.

Good partnership begins with listening: understanding what others are already doing, recognizing their strengths, and finding useful ways to come alongside.

For Benzi, that includes his long-standing work with InterVarsity and relationships with people helping leaders, organizations, and communities take their next steps. Each connection has its own focus, while creating room for encouragement, introductions, and thoughtful collaboration.

Explore the organizations below to learn about their work and discover where your interests, experience, or community might connect.

01 / CAMPUS MINISTRY

InterVarsity

Christian Fellowship/USA

Faith, community, and leadership on campus.

InterVarsity Christian Fellowship/USA is a Christian campus ministry serving students and faculty at colleges and universities across the United States. Its chapters create communities for exploring faith, studying Scripture, building friendships, and following Jesus across cultures and backgrounds.

Benzi’s connection is central to his ministry story. Since joining InterVarsity in 2011, he has served at the campus, area, regional, and national levels, helping equip leaders and support the wider field ministry.

Career Steps
Consulting

Practical guidance for what’s next.

Helping people and organizations move forward.

Career Steps Consulting provides career coaching, leadership development, workplace care, and business and technology consulting. Its work helps people clarify direction, navigate change, and connect practical plans with confident action.

Benzi’s relationship with Brent Kern connects complementary interests in leadership, organizational health, and service. Career Steps is also helping bring Benzi’s consulting and ministry story together through the design and ongoing care of this website.

03 / MISSION & SERVICE

Hope Sojourns

Purposeful journeys. Local relationships.

Connecting willing people with meaningful service.

Hope Sojourns brings individuals and teams alongside trusted local ministries through mission journeys and developing student internship opportunities. Its approach emphasizes cultural humility, listening to local leaders, practical service, and relationships that continue beyond a trip.

This connection creates opportunities to introduce churches, ministry teams, and individuals to service shaped by the priorities of local communities. It reflects a shared interest in helping people put faith into practice through encouragement, learning, and participation.
