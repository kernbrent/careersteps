About Career Steps

Experience translated into practical guidance.

Career Steps Consulting helps people and organizations find clarity, navigate complexity, and take purposeful action.

Our purpose

Helping people move forward—one clear step at a time.

Career and business challenges rarely arrive as tidy problems. They involve people, competing priorities, uncertainty, and decisions that carry real consequences. Career Steps creates room to examine the whole situation, name what matters most, and build a path that can actually be followed.

Our work spans individual coaching, leadership development, workplace relationships, organizational strategy, project delivery, digital experience, and facilitated corporate learning journeys.

The approach is direct, thoughtful, and grounded in respect for the people doing the work.

Brent Kern
Founder, Career Steps Consulting
