Client engagement

WeatherCall Services

Reducing technology continuity risk while preparing for growth.

Over a 10-month engagement, Career Steps partnered with four WeatherCall leaders to organize critical technology knowledge, strengthen delivery practices, coach leadership, and create a more durable operating foundation.

10 monthsEngagement duration
4 leadersLeadership partnership
CompleteDocumentation delivered

The challenge

Important system knowledge was concentrated with one senior technical leader. The codebase, security expectations, project practices, and institutional knowledge needed stronger organization and documentation.

The work

  • Organized the codebase and technical knowledge
  • Established security parameters
  • Introduced modern project-management practices
  • Coached executive leadership
  • Completed comprehensive codebase documentation

The outcome

WeatherCall gained stronger operating discipline, improved continuity and knowledge transfer, and a more credible technology and delivery foundation for pursuing larger corporate opportunities.

Founder-led initiative

The solution
